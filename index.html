How it comes together

AI for speed. Human hands for the finish.

AI tools handle the repetitive lift — transcripts, rough assembly, first-pass generation — so editing time goes into pacing, story, and grade instead of busywork.

02

03

Human edit & grade

Hands-on pacing, sound design, captions, and color grading — the craft AI alone can't finish.

04

Polish & deliver

Final review against the brief, export in the right formats, and delivery on schedule.
